Mount Kilimanjaro:

A massive dormant volcano and Africa's highest peak at 5,895 meters. It is famous because you can hike to the top without any technical climbing skills.

Mount Fuji:

Japan's highest peak at 3,776 meters. It features a stunning symmetrical cone and rich cultural history as a sacred pilgrimage site.

Mount Hoverla:

Hoverla is the highest mountain in Ukraine, standing at 2,061 meters (6,762 feet) above sea level.
